What you are looking at

S1 · Marquee HeroFull-bleed backdrop with a three-stop bottom scrim. The title is Fraunces 600 at −0.02em; the meta row is Inter 500, 11px, letterspaced caps.
S2 · Smoked-glass chromeGlass is rationed to five places only: nav bar, player chrome, sheet grabbers, the TV rail, and pairing panels. Low-tier devices swap it for elevated plus a hairline.
S3 · The copper threadExactly one copper element per region — the Play button, the progress fill, the active tab, the focus ring. Never two competing accents.
S4 · Reel labelsLetterspaced caps above a hairline rule, borrowed from film leader frames. Used for every shelf heading.
Ten-foot type floorOn TV nothing renders below 16sp and body text sits at 20sp or above. Focus is a 3dp copper ring with a 1.04 scale, not a colour change.
Honest statesEvery shelf has skeleton, empty and error variants. The player says “Refreshing the stream…” rather than pretending a dropped URL is still valid.
